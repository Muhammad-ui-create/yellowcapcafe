The friendly corner

A cafe on Sheridan Road

Yellow Cap Cafe is a small neighbourhood coffee shop in Rogers Park. The idea is a simple one: a short menu done properly, a room you are welcome to sit in for as long as you like, and staff who get to know the regulars.

The yellow cap is meant to read as a tip of the hat on the way past: come in, sit down, take your time.
