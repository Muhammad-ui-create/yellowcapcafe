The friendly corner

We are a small neighbourhood cafe with big windows and mismatched chairs. Beans roasted a few streets over, milk from the dairy on the hill, and a regulars' wall that keeps growing. Come in for a quick one at the counter or stay all morning with a book. Either way, we will know your order by Thursday.
